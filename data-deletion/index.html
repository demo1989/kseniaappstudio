Ksenia App Studio - Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like to request deletion of your personal data associated with Ksenia App Studio applications, please follow the instructions below.

How to request data deletion

  1. Send an email to: kseniaappstudio@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject: "Data Deletion Request".
  3. Please provide enough information for us to identify your account or data associated with the application.

After receiving your request, we will process the deletion of the applicable personal data associated with your account.

Contact: kseniaappstudio@gmail.com
